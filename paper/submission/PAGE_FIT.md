# Page-fit gate

The journal guide for an Original Paper is about 7 pages in this template. A paper 20% over that guide, 8.4 pages, is the hard ceiling. The filler was marked `filler` words, not manuscript text. `gate.tex` is generated by `scripts/page_fit_submission_v100.py` and is not part of the submission.

Three layouts were compiled at the full word budget:

- Six figures in the main text: 9 pages. This is over the ceiling.
- Figure 2 moved to the supplement: 8 pages. This is over the 7-page target and under the ceiling.
- Figures 2 and 6 moved to the supplement: 6 pages. This meets the target.

Decision: Figures 2 and 6 are supplementary. Figures 1, 3, 4 and 5 stay in the main text. The word budget is not cut. Restoring Figure 6 would put the same text at 8 pages, which is still under the ceiling, if a later draft has room.

Word budget:

- Abstract: 150 words at most.
- Introduction: 700.
- Methods, excluding the machine-learning subsection: 1,400.
- Machine-learning subsection: 300.
- Implementation: 350.
- Results: 1,200.
- Discussion: 600.

Section total, excluding the abstract and the captions: 4,550. Figure and table captions are extra. Budget about 45 words for a figure caption and 30 words for a table caption.
